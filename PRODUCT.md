# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and technical reviewers evaluating Jonathan for junior AI/ML and AI systems engineering roles. They need to understand his role fit, strongest evidence, and technical depth quickly, while technical peers should still be able to explore how the work was built.

## Product Purpose

Present Jonathan as an AI systems engineer who can take ambitious machine-learning ideas from research through implementation and deployment. Success means a visitor can identify the three strongest projects, understand their measurable outcomes, and reach Jonathan or his resume within 60 to 90 seconds without losing the memorable interactive experience.

## Brand Personality

Technical, cinematic, and precise. The experience should feel authored and engineering-led: confident enough to be expressive, disciplined enough to remain readable, responsive, and fast.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic developer templates, interchangeable SaaS card grids, decorative motion applied uniformly, terminal styling used without technical meaning, and portfolio experiences that hide evidence behind long intros or fragile GPU effects.

## Design Principles

- Lead with proof: measurable outcomes and working systems appear before broad claims.
- Earn the spectacle: expressive motion supports narrative transitions and technical identity.
- Offer two speeds: preserve discovery for technical peers while giving recruiters a fast path.
- Degrade with intent: lower-capability and reduced-motion experiences remain complete and designed.
- Keep contact close: role availability, resume, and direct contact stay easy to find.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, focus, landmarks, and keyboard operation. Respect reduced-motion preferences, avoid forced large-scale motion in degraded mode, maintain complete content without WebGL, and support touch and integrated-GPU devices without loss of functionality.
